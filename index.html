Step-by-step setup to connect your ESP32 desk bot to your phone hotspot.

Step 1 of 4

Enter Wi-Fi Setup Mode

Take your ESP32 desk bot and activate its setup receiver:

👆
Touch Sensor:

Touch and hold the touch sensor on the bot's head for 3 seconds.

✨ The screen will say: "WiFi setup mode" and broadcast an access point named EyesBot-Setup.

Step 2 of 4

Prepare Credentials in Memory

Because your phone will lose internet in the next step when connecting to the bot, enter your passphrase now to load credentials into memory.

✓ Credentials Ready in Offline Memory

Hotspot SSID and password have been decrypted into local RAM. You can safely disconnect from Wi-Fi.

Step 3 of 4

Connect to the Bot's Wi-Fi

  1. Open your phone's Wi-Fi Settings.
  2. Connect to the network: EyesBot-Setup (No password).
  3. Crucial: When your phone asks "No internet. Switch to mobile data?", choose "Stay Connected" or "Keep Wi-Fi".
  4. Return to this browser tab.
Step 4 of 4

Send to Bot

Tap the big button below to send your hotspot details directly to http://192.168.4.1/save.

What happens next:

1. The bot saves your credentials and restarts.
2. Turn on your phone's Portable Hotspot (2.4 GHz) so the bot connects!
