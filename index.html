Step 3 of 4

Connect to the Bot's Wi-Fi

Switch your phone's Wi-Fi to the bot's temporary setup network:

  1. Open your phone's Wi-Fi Settings.
  2. Connect to the network: EyesBot-Setup (No password needed).

  4. Return to this browser tab.

What happens next:

1. The bot saves your credentials to its memory and will immediately restart.
2. Turn on your phone's Portable Hotspot (2.4 GHz band) now so the bot can connect to it!

Desk Bot Notifications

Display live popups on your bot's OLED screen for WhatsApp and Instagram.

1. Your Bot's Local IP Address

Look at your desk bot's clock screen. The local IP address (e.g. 192.168.43.120) is shown at the bottom.

Both your phone and bot must be connected to the same Wi-Fi / hotspot.

2. Generated Webhook URLs

These are the HTTP GET endpoints your phone's automation app will trigger whenever a message arrives.

WhatsApp Template GET
http://BOT_IP/notify?app=whatsapp&from=NAME&msg=TEXT
Instagram Template GET
http://BOT_IP/notify?app=instagram&from=NAME&msg=TEXT
MacroDroid Ready-to-Paste URL:
http://BOT_IP/notify?app=whatsapp&from=[notification_title]&msg=[notification_text]

MacroDroid automatically replaces [notification_title] with sender name and [notification_text] with the message!

3. Setup in MacroDroid (Recommended & Free)

Follow these 3 easy steps to make your phone alert your bot automatically:

  1. Install MacroDroid: Download it free from the Google Play Store.
  2. Add Macro: Tap Add Macro and set name to "EyesBot Notifications".
  3. Trigger: Tap (+), choose Notification ➔ Notification Received ➔ Select WhatsApp and/or Instagram.
  4. Action: Tap (+), choose Connectivity ➔ HTTP Request.
    • Method: GET
    • URL: Paste the MacroDroid URL copied above.
  5. Save: Tap the checkmark icon to save. Whenever you receive a message, your desk bot will chirp and show the notification!

4. Test Notification

Test your desk bot right now to see the notification popup on its screen.

ℹ️ Important Compatibility Notes

  • Android Only: Automation tools like MacroDroid/Tasker can intercept notifications on Android. Apple iOS does not allow background notification interception.
  • ASCII English Only: The ESP32 OLED screen firmware displays standard English letters and symbols. Emojis and non-English scripts are stripped or not shown.

Privacy & Account

Your data ownership and zero-knowledge encryption safeguards.

🔐 Zero-Knowledge Security

EyesBot Hub is designed with privacy-first client-side cryptography:

  • Hotspot passwords are encrypted with AES-GCM (256-bit) directly in your browser.
  • The encryption key is derived using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and 250,000 rounds from your secret passphrase.
  • Your secret passphrase is never sent over the network or saved anywhere. Only you can unlock your password.
  • Hosted free with HTTPS encryption on GitHub Pages. No third-party analytics or trackers.
Reminder:

Only store your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot password. Never store banking credentials, master passwords, or primary Wi-Fi keys.
